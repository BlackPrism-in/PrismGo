PrismGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

PrismGo is a vehicle request and tracking app for organisations. Employees request a vehicle, their head of department approves it, and the transport team assigns a vehicle and driver and follows the trip. PrismGo is built and operated by BlackPrism (blackprism.in) for the organisation that gives you access, usually your employer.

Information we collect

  • Account details entered by your organisation's administrator: your name, mobile number, email address (optional), department and role.
  • Trip requests: pickup and drop addresses and coordinates, times, number of people, purpose, notes, approvals and comments.
  • Vehicle locations from the GPS trackers fitted to your organisation's vehicles, recorded during trips. PrismGo does not use your phone's location.
  • Drivers: name, phone number, licence number and expiry, entered by the transport team.
  • Device and usage data: a push notification token for your device, login codes (valid for 5 minutes), sign-in sessions, and server logs such as IP address and request times, used for security.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code.
  • To create, approve, assign and track vehicle requests.
  • To send you updates about your requests and trips in the app, by push notification and on WhatsApp.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

  • People in your organisation who need it: your head of department and the transport team see your requests; drivers receive the trip details and your name and phone number for pickup.
  • Service providers that run PrismGo on our behalf: Meta (WhatsApp messages, including login codes), Google Firebase (push notifications), our hosting providers (Render, Vercel), and the GPS tracking provider that supplies vehicle locations.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account and trip records are kept while your organisation uses PrismGo, so trips can be reviewed later. Login codes expire after 5 minutes. When your organisation removes your account or stops using PrismGo, we delete or anonymise the data, except where we must keep it by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is limited by role: requesters see their own requests, heads of department see their department's, and only the transport team and administrators see all requests and vehicle locations.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information through your organisation's PrismGo administrator, or by writing to us. You can turn off push notifications in your phone's settings.

Delete your PrismGo account and data

To have your PrismGo account and data deleted:

  1. Ask your organisation's PrismGo administrator (usually the transport team), or email admin@blackprism.in with the subject "PrismGo data deletion" from your work email, giving your name, mobile number and organisation.
  2. We confirm the request with your organisation and reply within 30 days.

We delete your name, mobile number, email address, device push tokens and sign-in sessions. Trip records you were part of are kept for your organisation's records with your name removed, unless the law requires us to keep them as they are. Server logs are deleted after 90 days.

Children

PrismGo is for employees and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above, and tell your organisation about significant changes.
